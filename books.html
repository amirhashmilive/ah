Johar Gandhi – A Research Publication on Tribal and Women Freedom Fighters of Chhattisgarh

Johar Gandhi is a comprehensive research publication highlighting the role of India's tribal and women freedom fighters from Chhattisgarh. Released on October 2, 2022, by Honorable Chief Minister Bhupesh Baghel. The book features 13 chapters documenting unsung heroes of the independence movement from the tribal heartland.
