Break Up | When love fades, and life takes over

Hindi Short Film

Arjun is about to meet Riya for the last time, a final confrontation before he marries someone else. Heartbroken and angry, Riya struggles to comprehend how their love story turned into a mere chapter of his past. A heated exchange unfolds, each word laden with the weight of their shared history. Unspoken Goodbye is a poignant exploration of love, loss, and the complex interplay of personal choices and societal pressures.

Transforming Rural Livelihoods – Chhattisgarh High-Impact Mega Watershed Project

Rural Development Documentary • 2018

A groundbreaking initiative covering 6.9 lakh hectares across 26 blocks in 12 districts of Chhattisgarh. A collaboration between Government of Chhattisgarh, Bharat Rural Livelihoods Foundation (BRLF), Axis Bank Foundation, and Ford Foundation. Launched on October 5, 2018, this four-year project aims to uplift 100,000 small and marginal households through watershed management, soil conservation, and improved farm productivity. Aligned with the NARWA treatment program of the state government.

Partners: MGNREGA, BRLF, Axis Bank Foundation, Ford Foundation, CSO Partners
Coverage: 12 districts, 26 blocks, 6.9 lakh hectares catchment area
Beneficiaries: 100,000 small and marginal households
