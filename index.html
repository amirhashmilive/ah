Bolti Nadi – Save River Documentary
A documentary following Amir Hashmi's 90-kilometer journey along the Sakri River in Chhattisgarh. The river had been dry for nearly 30 years. This film documents the revival efforts, community mobilization, and environmental restoration. A landmark initiative for river conservation in India.
