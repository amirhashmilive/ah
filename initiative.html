Bolti Nadi – River Revival Movement

Founded in 2016. A 90-kilometer river walk from Kariyama Village (Kabirdham) to Darhi Village (Bemetara) documenting the revival of Sakri River after 30 years of drought. Community mobilization, silt evacuation, and water restoration efforts. Supported by Chief Ministers of Chhattisgarh.
