Amir Hashmi – Complete Music Collection

Complete music collection featuring Hindustani classical, Sufi, and original compositions. Amir Hashmi holds a Sangeet Visharad (6-year diploma) from Indira Kala Sangit Vishwavidyalaya, Khairagarh. Trained in Hindustani Classical Vocals.

Featured Album: Kyu Aisa Hota Hain (2015)

A tribute album for Nepal Earthquake victims. Launched by Mr. Pramod Dubey. Received international appreciation from Brazil, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and Singapore. Featured on the front page of Dainik Bhaskar (June 9, 2015).
